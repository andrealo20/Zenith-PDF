# Privacy

Zenith PDF works on your computer. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it sends nothing
about you or your documents to anyone, the author included.

## What stays on your computer

- Your PDF files and everything you add to them: annotations, notes, stamps, signatures, audio notes.
- The settings, the list of recent files, tags and stars, the templates and the markup library.
- The index that makes "search inside every PDF" work, and the thumbnails of the home page, kept in the
  system's cache folder for your user.
- Text recognition, spelling, read aloud and the assistant, which run on your computer with the engines and
  dictionaries your system already has.

Opening an Office file runs Microsoft Word or LibreOffice on your computer, if one is installed, to turn it
into a PDF.

## The network

- The copy from the Microsoft Store never connects to the internet by itself: the Store installs its updates.
- The copy downloaded from GitHub asks GitHub once a day whether a newer version is out, if "Check for updates"
  is on in Settings, and downloads it if "Install updates automatically" is on. The request carries no
  information about you or your files beyond what any web request carries, such as your IP address, which
  GitHub handles under its own privacy statement.
- A link in a document opens in your browser only when you click it; Zenith PDF asks first, unless you
  told it not to.

## The microphone and the camera

Zenith PDF uses the microphone only while you record an audio note, and the camera only when you take a
picture to put on a page. Both start only when you ask, and what they record goes only into your document.
You can turn off their access in Windows Settings > Privacy & security.
